POCKET FIELD
Make a sound map
5 minutes · still
Bring: Paper and pencil, optional

1. Find a comfortable outdoor spot and put your screen away.
2. Listen for a nearby sound, a distant sound, and one that changes.
3. Draw three marks on paper in the directions they came from.

Choose a familiar permitted place and comfortable conditions.
No route, weather, medical or species-identification advice.

Close the screen. The rest happens outside.
